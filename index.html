Grace Luo
I am a second year PhD student at BAIR advised by Professor Trevor Darrell. My research interests lie in multimodal vision and language models. Recently, I’ve been interested in analyzing the internal representations of generative models, including controlling their properties and distilling them for discriminative tasks.
