Grace Luo
I'm a final-year PhD student at BAIR advised by Professor Trevor Darrell. Before that, I received my B.S. and M.S. degrees in EECS at UC Berkeley.
My research is on interpreting and controlling generative models. For example, I've worked on re-purposing image generators for computer vision tasks, and meta-modeling language activations for better LLM probing and steering.
